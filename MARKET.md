# Why an arrow-escape game?

Research snapshot: 11 November 2026.

AppBrain's current India free-puzzle chart and its arrow-puzzle topic page show strong current activity in the arrow-escape format. The Google Play listing for Arrow Puzzle demonstrates the underlying casual tap-to-clear category. Arrow Room uses an independently implemented puzzle engine, generated levels, icon and visual design; no competitor artwork, code or level content was copied.

- https://www.appbrain.com/stats/google-play-rankings/top_free/puzzle/in
- https://www.appbrain.com/topics/arrow-puzzle-games
- https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.easybrain.arrow.puzzle.game

The practical case for a solo developer is a small offline game, short sessions, generated/verified content and natural optional hint/revive placements. The daily board gives a reason to return. These are design hypotheses to validate with real players; chart success by another publisher does not predict installs, retention or revenue for a new app.
